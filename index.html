About these datasets and how to read the maps

Dates are the service request’s creation date, as recorded by the city. Each point represents a report, not necessarily a unique incident. Comparison month ranges include both selected months. Custom date ranges include both selected dates. The archive spans 2014–2025. Not all categories have records in every year; names and reporting practices can change. This is a fixed snapshot, not a live feed.

Grid sizes are approximate ground dimensions near Austin, from 10 × 10 m (100 m²) to 1,000 × 1,000 m (1 km²). Cells keep the same origin when dates or datasets change. Comparison panels share one color scale. Single-map density colors use the largest cell count in the selected period. Share mode shows reports per cell as a percentage of all selected reports in that period; it does not adjust for population. Dots can overlap; click one to choose among reports at that location. Each issue has its own color. With all issues selected, density-cell hue shows the most frequent issue in that cell; opacity shows volume. Click a cell for the issue breakdown. Total counts include every record.

Only records with coordinates within 30.0–30.7° N and 98.1–97.3° W are mapped. Omission counts are listed above. Background roads and water are City of Austin Street Centerline and Lakes and Rivers snapshots downloaded on 6 October 2026. Major roads appear at city scale; zoom in for local streets and street names. This is a simplified reference map, not a historical basemap. Both the report CSVs and background geography are served from this project; no Austin API, external map tiles, or API key is used. No data upload is needed. Report choices are subsets of Austin 311 Public Data; the background geography comes from separate City of Austin GIS datasets.
